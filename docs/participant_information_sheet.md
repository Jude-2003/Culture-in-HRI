## What is the purpose of the study?
 
This is a student research project conducted as part of a postgraduate degree.
 
Robots are increasingly being used in public settings such as hospitals, hotels, and schools. Most research on how people interact with robots has focused on Western participants, meaning we know relatively little about how people from different cultural backgrounds respond to robot behaviour. This study investigates whether cultural background influences how people feel about interacting with a social robot.

## Why have I been invited?
 
You have been invited because we are looking for participants who are 18 years or older, university students, able to speak and understand English, and are from the United Kingdom or Saudi Arabia. You do not need any special skills or prior experience with robots to take part in this study. In total, the study aims to invite 10 participants.
 
---
 
## What will happen to me if I take part?
 
The study will last about 30 minutes and will only be conducted once. You will:
 
- Read and sign a consent form.
- Complete a short pre-task questionnaire (age, cultural background, gender, studies, prior robot experience).
- Complete a one-session Unity-based game while interacting with the robot in one of two randomly assigned experimental studies. You will be assigned to one of two games: a city builder game or an object sorting game. The robot will provide instructions and guidance throughout the game, and you will complete the game following the robot's guidance and interacting with the game on a computer.
- Finish with a post-task questionnaire about your experience and your view of the robot.
- For analysing our data, we will store both the game performance metrics and the questionnaire answers. Both will be stored securely and pseudonymised. Once the research project is submitted, research data will be anonymised.
Everything will be conducted at the Imperial College London White City campus.

## Transparency Notice
 
### How will we use information about you?
 
Imperial College London is the sponsor for this study and will act as the Data Controller. Personal data will be kept for 10 years after the study has finished. The study is expected to finish in 09/2026.
 
Information collected will include: name, contact details, age range, gender, and field and level of education. Your data will have a code number instead of your name.
 
Data will be protected by:
 
- Storage in ISO 27001 certified and/or Cyber Essentials accredited environments
- Robust pseudonymisation to prevent identification
- Access controls so only key personnel can access the data
### Legal Basis
 
Our legal basis for using your information under GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 is "performance of a task carried out in the public interest."
