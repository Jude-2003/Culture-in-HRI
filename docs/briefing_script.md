# Briefing Script
 
*Read aloud to the participant before the session begins. Maintain a neutral, friendly tone throughout. Do not reveal which condition the participant is in.*
 
---
 
Thank you for coming in today. Before we start, I want to tell you a little bit about what's going to happen.
 
This session is part of a research project I'm conducting for my master's at Imperial College. We're interested in how people interact with social robots, and specifically how people from different cultural backgrounds respond to different robot communication styles.
 
The session has three parts. First, you'll fill in a short questionnaire on this device — it should take about five minutes. It covers some background information about you and a few questions about your personal values.
 
After that, you'll play a little game with this robot. The robot will guide you through a short game on the screen. There are no right or wrong answers — just engage with it naturally, as you would with any person or device giving you instructions. The task should take around ten minutes.
 
Finally, you'll fill in another short questionnaire about your experience with the robot. That should take another five to ten minutes.
 
In total the session should take around twenty-five to thirty minutes.
 
A few things to note before we begin. Your participation is entirely voluntary — you can stop at any time and that won't affect you in any way. Your responses will be anonymous, and your data will only ever be reported as part of a group, never individually.
 
Do you have any questions before we start?
 
> *[Answer any questions. If asked about the specific purpose of the robot conditions, say: "I'll be able to tell you more about that at the end of the session."]*
 
Great. Whenever you're ready, we can begin with the first questionnaire.
